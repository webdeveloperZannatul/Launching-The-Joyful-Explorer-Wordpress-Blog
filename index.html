The Purpose

I've reached a point in life where I finally have the time to travel more freely. This blog is a way to keep a record of where I go, what I see, and what I experience along the way.

I've already found that places can blur together if I don't write them down. It's also a way for friends to follow along, and maybe find ideas for their own trips.

This is simply a personal journal—notes from the road as I go. If you're reading this, I'm glad you're here.
